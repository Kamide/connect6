Connect6 is a two-player strategy game similar to Gomoku, where players take turns placing black and white stones on a
board. The first player to get six or more stones in a row, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, wins. Black places
one stone on the first turn, while each player places two stones per turn afterward. Try it out at the following link
and see who wins!

https://kamide.github.io/connect6/
